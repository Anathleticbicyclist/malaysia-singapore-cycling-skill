## 一、KLIA2 大件寄存（WhatsApp 英文，发给 +6012 678 3034）

```text
Hi, I would like to store 3 bicycle boxes at your counter.

Details:
- Item: 3 cardboard bike boxes (road bikes, packed)
- Size per box: approx 115 x 80 x 28 cm (L x H x W), total 223 cm
- Weight: approx 18-20 kg each
- Storage period: 25 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027 (about 9 days / 10 x 24h)
- Pick-up: possibly early morning on 3 Jan, before 06:00

Questions:
1. Can you accept boxes of this size? Any maximum length limit?
2. Price per item per 24 hours for oversized items? Is there a lower rate after day 7?
3. Any package price for 3 items together?
4. Is early-morning pick-up (before 06:00) possible? What is the latest time to collect the night before?
5. Do I need to pay a deposit?

Please confirm in writing. Thank you.
```

## 三、酒店代存（英文邮件）

```text
Subject: Enquiry – luggage storage for 3 bike boxes (non-resident period)

Dear Front Desk,

We are 3 cyclists arriving on 25 Dec 2026 and departing 3 Jan 2027.
We would like to stay with you on the night of 25 Dec only, and store
3 packed bicycle boxes (approx 115 x 80 x 28 cm each, 18-20 kg each)
from 26 Dec 2026 until 2 Jan 2027 (about 7 days), while we are cycling
to Singapore and back.

Could you confirm:
1. Do you offer luggage storage for guests who are not staying during
   the storage period?
2. Is there a size limit or fee per item per day?
3. Is the storage room accessible at 22:00-23:00 on 2 Jan?

If this is not possible, we understand. Thank you.
```

## 五、青岛航空特殊行李（中文电话话术）

```text
您好，我购买了 QW907（青岛—吉隆坡）经济舱机票，想咨询运动自行车托运。

物品是 1 个自行车纸箱，装公路车，尺寸约 115×80×28cm，三边和约 223cm，
重量约 20kg。你们官网写明"运动用自行车"只能作为托运行李运输，
但国际航线每件三边和不得超过 158cm。

想确认五件事：
1. 这类超尺寸自行车箱能不能随这班 A321 客机走？
2. 需要提前办"特殊行李/超规行李"申请吗？提前几天、找谁办？
3. 超规费多少钱，怎么计费？
4. 单件重量上限还是 23kg 吗？超重怎么算？
5. 我们三人同行、同航班、同时办值机，免费行李额可以合并计算，
   三件一起办怎么计费？

麻烦告知，并请提供工号，我值机时可能需要核对。
```

## 六、发送纪律

1. 一次问全，不要分五条消息追问。
2. 必须要求**书面回复**（截图留存）。
3. 记录：发送时间、对方名称、答复内容、有效期。
4. 报价过期（尤其汇率与旺季附加费）要重问。
